Courtyard & Co.

Dining

One kitchen, open from 07:00 to 22:30, built around a courtyard and an old mango tree. Residents and walk-ins both welcome.

A short menu, cooked properly

Our chef, Rumana Kabir, spent nine years between Dhaka and Penang and the menu shows it. Twelve dishes at a time, half of them Bengali, written on the board each morning after the Karwan Bazar run. If something is not on it, it is because it was not good enough that day.

Breakfast is included for suite guests and ৳850 for everyone else. The bar opens at five.
